# A2 real point/vector source evidence

[Audit](audit.md) records the concrete use-case dispositions and proof limits.
[Results](results.json) retain two final matching-driver runs, each with fourteen
small source cases, nineteen sensitive controls and eight successful legacy
rewrites. All actual source geometry and descendants are independently checked
against owner boxes. Ordinal design plans are design evidence, not replacement
archive or viewer acceptance.

The final portable and native raw receipts are losslessly retained as
`portable-raw.json.gz` and `native-raw.json.gz`, with original and stored hashes
in results.json. [Source/artifact identities](source-artifacts.json) pin the
93 frozen production inputs, binaries, executed probe, wrapper and independent
meshoptimizer reference codec. No binaries or generated archives are committed.

The final external artifact directories end in `real-portable-final2` and
`real-native-final2`. Replay commands and bounded resource/CPU settings are in
the audit. The driver uses one converter at a time, lower priority, two Rayon
threads and explicit vector `--jobs 2`. Actual b3dm coverage is a trivial
BATCH_LENGTH-zero wrapper; broader tables/RTC semantics remain unproved.
Terminal external-template, imported metadata/resource/provenance, one-Attempt
lifecycle, installed interfaces and viewer acceptance remain separate gates.
