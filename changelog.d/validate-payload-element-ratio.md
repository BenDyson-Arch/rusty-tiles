### Changed

- `validate`: the default `totalPayloadElements` budget is now 16 million + 105 × stored bytes, up from max(16 million, 16 × stored bytes). The report's `limitDerivation` entry for it gains `"additive": true`. Indexed HLOD coarse tiles take fewer stored bytes for the same validation work. The native HLOD delivery check now charges its scratch-store rows (128 units), original vertex rows (16) and placed original keys (32) at their measured cost. The ratio gives every legitimate producer output measured a 1.5× margin: the densest, thousands of isolated tiny untextured triangles, needs about 70 units per stored byte. At the worst measured cost per unit, the admitted hostile work is at most 0.18 s plus 1,166 s of CPU per stored GB on a P-core and 0.24 s plus 1,575 s on an E-core (see the scratch-row entry).
- `validate`: the HLOD original-vertex alias check uses a spill sequence and one bounded external sort instead of the SQLite scratch store. This is about 3× faster on leaf-heavy archives, and the verdicts are unchanged, with one exception. An archive with both an alias conflict and a non-finite placed position is now always refused for the non-finite position (`interfaceSegments`). Before, the refusal came from whichever the scan reached first. Both are invalid verdicts.
