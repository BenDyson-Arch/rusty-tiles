### Changed

- `validate`: the default `totalPayloadElements` budget is now 16 million + 130 × stored bytes, up from max(16 million, 16 × stored bytes). The report's `limitDerivation` entry for it gains `"additive": true`. Indexed HLOD coarse tiles take fewer stored bytes for the same validation work. The native HLOD delivery check now charges its scratch-store rows (128 units), original vertex rows (16) and placed original keys (32) at their measured cost. The ratio gives every legitimate producer output measured a 1.5× margin: the densest, thousands of untextured primitives of tiny isolated or repeated triangles, needs up to 86.6 units per stored byte. At the worst measured cost per unit, the admitted hostile work is at most 0.18 s plus 1,492 s of CPU per stored GB on a P-core and 0.24 s plus 1,989 s on an E-core (see the scratch-row entry), above the C2 statement's 1,037 and 1,875 s.
- `validate`: the HLOD original-vertex alias check uses a spill sequence and one bounded external sort instead of the SQLite scratch store. This is about 3× faster on leaf-heavy archives, and the verdicts are unchanged, with one exception. An archive with both an alias conflict and a non-finite placed position is now always refused for the non-finite position (`interfaceSegments`). Before, the refusal came from whichever the scan reached first. Both are invalid verdicts.
