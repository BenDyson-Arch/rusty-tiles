`f1c1_oracle.py` checks the [F1c1 contract](../docs/architecture/f1c1-contract.md) using only Python's standard library and earlier independently authored F1a/F1b/F1b2/F1b3 fixture/container/resource truth. It imports no production decoder, validator, writer or coordinate helper. Forward WGS84 truth comes from NGA constants, independently authored Decimal80 Taylor sin/cos, exact cardinal trigonometry and Hamilton quaternion action. No inverse round trip or native/portable agreement supplies coordinate truth.

The frozen [analytic references](fixtures/f1c1/analytic-references.json) predate production replay. Their 21 anchors include cardinals, both antimeridian endpoints and nearby values, exact/near poles with nonzero meridians, elevated and negative heights, ±50,000,000m heights, and forward Earth-centre/near-centre positions. Literal pole bases and an exact rational matrix for `q=(1,2,3,4)/sqrt30` self-test sign, handedness and noncommuting orientation. References record their mathematical authoring provenance. `--write-references` is an explicit authoring operation; acceptance recomputes and compares the frozen file instead of silently replacing it.

There are 34 independently authored source variants: all 26 prior core-PBR companion/resource cases plus instancing, nested source translation, near-1e6 local magnitudes, zero/line/plane extents, thin/spike geometry and three asymmetric consumer triangles. The fixture manifest records exact source/dependency SHA256/lengths and independently specified placements. The 33 placement requests exercise Local, the 21 anchors, mixed quaternion plus post-node source Y-up offset, q/−q, large cancelling-sign offsets, explicit identity, just-inside quaternion normalization, and ±90° around each ENU axis.

The production plan runs every placement with all-slot geometry at leaf ceilings 1,3,1000, every source variant under mixed placement at those ceilings, and five near-limit geometry/budget cases. Positive conversion counts are derived from receipts, rather than promoted from the plan. Offset and height budget boundaries use the contract's exact operative cap `58_998_676.192_431_12`: immediately-above values and a cap plus a minimum subnormal must be refused. Fraction arithmetic independently establishes that rounded f64 summation cannot be the admission oracle. Request refusals cover every numeric component's NaN/±infinity, angle ranges, zero/nonunit/enormous quaternions, anchor-less orientation/offset combinations, height/offset/combined/cancellation budget limits and precise mixed-sum boundaries. Each CLI refusal is replayed with source present and absent to establish request-validation precedence and no staging/output-parent creation. Three malformed CLI tuple-length cases separately require Clap's pre-producer usage transport (`error.code=usage`, no job-kind field, exit2). Numeric and combination refusals retain exact typed job kinds. Python tuple arity follows its separately tested PyO3 TypeError/ValueError transport.

## Original-byte local and world proof

The existing resource inspector has a strict `placement_expectation` seam. Its default remains Local with an explicit exact identity root, schema4/profile `f1c1-placed-gltf-v1`, source interpretation `local-gltf` and normalized typed placement/report fields. Earth expectations come solely from independent requests and Decimal arithmetic. Original archive CRC, 3TZ index, exact resource/leaf/member closure and report remain checked. No archive is stripped or repacked to make old assertions pass. Existing local oriented-corner/material/UV/color/normal/tangent comparisons remain separate from world proof.

The new world reader applies decoded glTF node transforms, glTF Y-up→tile Z-up once, then the entire parent*child tile transform chain. A literal synthetic fixture with nonidentity source GLB node and nonidentity child transform establishes the reader's complete ordering even though accepted production leaves have no extra transform. Root and leaf centres and half axes become world OBBs; every decoded vertex and every descendant-box corner must be contained. Degenerate extents use projection on the rigid unit basis, avoiding inversion of singular half-axis matrices. Local bounds use zero epsilon; world calculations use the contract's separate 1e-6m f64 allowance.

Every stored local corner's position is transformed by independent Decimal80 placement and compared with complete candidate-world arithmetic. Normals and tangent XYZ are independently rotated, and rigid placement must preserve tangent W. Source f32 POSITION/companion fidelity is established first using the prior local tolerances; no Earth-scale component enters the f32 storage-error formula. POSITION/resource member hashes must remain unchanged across placement choices for the same source/leaf ceiling. Leaf GE0 and the local conservative diagonal routing metric remain unchanged under rigid placement; this is selection evidence, not a measured approximation or source-to-storage Hausdorff claim.

CRC-valid independently authored positive archives pass before mutations are tested. Controls reject missing/corrupt/transposed/double-axis root matrices, wrong height, report/root disagreement, anchor/offset/quaternion/interpretation corruption, undersized/moved boxes, incorrect routing error and extra child transforms. Separate positive/negative zero/line/plane OBB probes establish degenerate containment sensitivity. Prior PBR/resource sensitive controls still run in their original self-tests.

## Unchanged-root actual consumer evidence

`f1c1_viewer.py` loads candidate tilesets with their original emitted root matrix and identity `tiles.modelMatrix`. Cameras and three noncollinear interior targets are fixed from independently computed Decimal world positions; neither an output bounding sphere nor a Cesium geodetic/frame helper defines the expected location. Cases use Brisbane/western anchors, a nonzero longitude at the pole, mixed active quaternion/offsets and 3cm triangles at a 50-million-metre anchor. Post-render ordinary-frame leaf/command/triangle counts, owned scene picks, far suppression and return-to-near must pass. Each wrong-axis, inverse orientation/order, wrong-height and missing-root control must miss at least one fixed target; the zero-root-error control must suppress all leaves. Pick rendering changes Cesium traversal statistics, so ordinary-frame statistics are captured before the narrow-frustum pick passes.

```sh
python3 -S -B tests/f1c1_viewer.py --binary /absolute/frozen/rusty-tiles \
  --cesium-dir /existing/node_modules/cesium/Build/Cesium \
  --node-modules /existing/node_modules --chromium /existing/chrome \
  --json-output /tmp/f1c1-viewer.json
```

`--synthetic` independently authors its tilesets/leaf assets without a converter. It establishes consumer feasibility and sensitivity only; its receipt explicitly lacks a production binary SHA. This mode passed locally during authoring with Cesium1.146.0/Playwright1.63.0 and Chrome153.0.8010.12/SwiftShader. Correct cases rendered all three leaves/triangles and picked all fixed targets; every placement corruption picked zero targets. Measured depth-point errors include the finite raster pixel centre: approximately0.011–0.0175m for metre-scale triangles and0.000158–0.000212m for the small-detail probe. Those observations are not a millimetre universal viewer claim or the offline arithmetic acceptance threshold. Later candidate replay must use the final frozen binary.

The existing Three0.180 lit PBR comparison and earlier Cesium Local routing/unlit checks remain separate regressions. This browser driver does not claim a new material appearance proof. Package/runtime/driver/reference hashes, binary identity, actual receipts and final nonauthor review bind evidence to the candidate. Hosted platform/native/wheel/official Blender CI, lifecycle/resource replay, general CRS/datum/epoch conversion, picking identities, approximation and implicit delivery are outside this driver and must not be inferred from its success.

The first replay exposed duplicate output filenames for independently planned scenarios; CreateNew correctly returned output conflict. Neither failure was a production placement defect; numeric and combination failure expectations were retained and strengthened to exit2. Final receipts pin the corrected driver hashes.
